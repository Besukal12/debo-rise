We start small. We aim high.

We build each step only after the one before it works.

The roadmap

Pilot (months 0 to 3)

Telegram community, 3 fields, about 30 students, and a Showcase Day. We prove that students finish real projects.

Light website (months 3 to 6)

Student profiles and project pages. A second group starts.

MVP app (months 6 to 12)

A portfolio app where mentors verify projects and companies can offer internships.

Full platform (year 2 and beyond)

A student community across Ethiopia with feeds, chat, points, trophies, and live mentoring.

The app

Telegram is our free place for chat. The app focuses on what Telegram cannot do: verified proof of skills.

MVP features

  • Student profile and project pages
  • Progress through Learn, Think, Build, Solve, Teach
  • Mentor feedback and verified badge
  • One shareable portfolio link
  • Company view for internships

Full vision

  • Feed to share work, plus chat and groups
  • Points, trophies, and challenges
  • Learning tracks for every field
  • Live mentoring and recordings
  • School dashboards and mobile apps

Why not build it all now?

An app with no users is empty. We first learn what students need, then build only that.

How it can grow

  • Schools join and support the program
  • Companies sponsor challenges and offer internships
  • Optional paid workshops later
