Six weeks. One real project.

Pick a week to see what each field does. Every week ends with something you can show.

Your week

  • Meeting A: in person at school, 60 minutes
  • Meeting B: online, 45 minutes
  • Every day: 1 to 2 hours of learning and practice
  • Daily proof: post a screenshot or note in your group
  • Day 7: rest

Stuck? Call a debo.

If you are stuck for 20 minutes, post #CallADebo with a screenshot and your question. Classmates and mentors gather to help you finish. Helping others is practice for the Teach step.

How you earn the certificate

The certificate is signed by the school and Debo Rise. You earn it by finishing, not only by attending.

  • Attend at least 9 of 12 meetings
  • Post proof of work at least 20 times
  • Finish your final project and pass the mentor review
  • Present at Showcase Day

Showcase Day

At the end of Week 6, you show your work to the principal, teachers, parents, and mentors. Some students present on stage. Everyone has a station to explain their project.

What you leave with

  • One practical skill
  • One finished real project
  • A portfolio piece and mentor feedback
  • A plan for your next step
